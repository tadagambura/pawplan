# PawPlan

**Your pet's care, all in one place**

> #14 of 17 · Wave 2: Recurring keepers · Used: Daily · Harder · Revenue potential: Medium · Family, Pets & Plants

## What it is

Feeding, walks, flea and worming treatments, vaccinations, vet appointments, medication and weight, with reminders and a shared log so the whole household knows whether the dog's been fed.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Pet owners spend freely and treatments recur monthly, which gives natural retention. Shared households add users. Pet insurance and food are strong affiliate categories.

## Who it's for

Dog and cat owners, multi-pet households.

**The problem:** 'Has the dog been fed?', forgotten flea treatments and missed vaccinations.

## Version one

- Pet profiles
- Recurring treatment and vaccination reminders
- Feeding and walk log
- Vet appointments and medication
- Weight tracking

**Example:** Two flatmates see the dog's breakfast has been logged, and both get the monthly flea-treatment reminder.

## Later

- Shared household log (sync)
- Vet record export
- Multiple pets

## Why people come back

Built on **The Progression Loop**, the portfolio's shared retention framework
(streaks with forgiveness, visible progress, milestones, recaps, no guilt-trips).

| Stage | In PawPlan |
|---|---|
| Cue | Treatment and feeding reminders |
| Tiny win | Log a feed, walk or treatment |
| Progress | Care-on-track status; walk streaks |
| Share | Pet milestone cards |
| Next step | The next treatment is scheduled automatically |
| Cadence | Daily/monthly |

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- Fed: the pet does a happy wiggle
- Walk logged: the pet drags Kaos along on the lead
- Treatment done: the pet puts on a tiny superhero cape
- Pet's birthday: party hat, tail-chasing
- Weight logged: the pet stands proudly on the scale and flexes

**Line we don't cross:** These are real animals: never animate illness, injury or death.

## AI

**None.** Not needed. Plain code does everything this app does.

## Watch-outs

No veterinary advice: always signpost to a vet. Sharing needs a backend.
